Meet Colin Newman

Local service. Serious know-how.

Colin Newman, owner of Rinks and Links, has more than 25 years of experience sharpening skates and servicing equipment. After a career in sporting goods retail, he and his wife moved to Collingwood and turned that know-how into a small shop built on personal service.

Whether it is hockey players gearing up for a game or golfers looking for a better feel on the club, Colin takes pride in work that keeps people performing at their best — one pair of skates or one set of clubs at a time.
